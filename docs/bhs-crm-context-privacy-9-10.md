## 9. Separate sales follow-up from brand health

The existing brief form concatenates card notes and dated interaction type/note entries into `meetingNotes`. Those raw notes previously entered the analyst's initial message. A CRM failure to reach a prospect could therefore be read as a brand decision-making or operating weakness.

The model-facing copy now removes clear sales-follow-up-only sentences identified from the CRM's actual dated/type format and unambiguous first-person prospect-contact wording. A mixed line keeps its business goal, partner, channel or scope clause. Ambiguous communication statements and actual reported customer-service problems remain available; they are not removed by a general communication keyword rule. The analyst and same-conversation reviewer are explicitly told that our sales process is not a brand defect and that an unclear subject cannot support a negative brand conclusion.

Brand, goals, needs, service discussion, ownership/decision context, partners, countries, relevant meeting content, budgets, dates and product identifiers remain in the same natural conversation. No second classifier/model, mandatory topic checklist, CRM UI change or CRM-data write is added. Semantic interpretation remains the analyst's responsibility and requires the user's later real-output acceptance.

## 10. Project a safe model copy before counting or generation

`business-brief-private-input-v1` creates an allowlisted model brief without changing the original snapshot. Common explicit credential assignments, quoted passwords/passphrases, recognized API/token/key formats, private keys, credential-bearing/signed URLs and private contact email/phone values are removed from that copy. Public business URLs and legitimate service descriptions such as password-reset work remain usable. Numeric goals, budgets, dates and explicit SKU/EAN/GTIN identifiers are preserved. Arbitrary unlabeled secrets cannot be perfectly classified by deterministic rules; ambiguous business text is retained rather than silently deleting the brand's useful context.

A free durable model-input checkpoint binds the safe copy and its version/digest to the original canonical brief digest. Only safe data and redaction/CRM-exclusion counts are saved in that new checkpoint; removed literal values are not stored there. The original one-brief/one-budget context, CRM records, source archives and previously owned receipts remain intact. A lost free checkpoint recovers on the same account before any provider work.

Protection applies before Claude token counting/generation, to new and legacy/frozen conversation text, Gemini helper questions, the complete fallback brief/history, and writing-only recovery. Safe opaque thinking, native tool identities and source passages remain intact. Published public-source contact text is retained as evidence rather than incorrectly masking its passage offsets. If a source body or signed-thinking block contains unsafe credentials that cannot be changed without breaking evidence/signature integrity, fresh provider dispatch is stopped instead of silently altering that proof. A credential-bearing brief website is not silently changed to a different research URL. Such refusals keep the established honest partial/brief-based offer handoff and do not trigger automatic paid fallback research.

Completed/uncertain paid receipts are checked before privacy enrichment and retain ownership. An unowned native request whose input changes is counted again using only the safe input, retains its chosen output limit and reserves that actual safe envelope before generation. Gemini historical presentation/layout checks still run before any new count/generation. Private research queries are refused before reserve/FX/provider dispatch. No new budget or higher cap is introduced; all work and infrastructure stay in the original 5,000-kuruş account.
