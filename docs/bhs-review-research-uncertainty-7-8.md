## 8. Keep our knowledge limits separate from brand conditions

New source archives and source presentations carry an evidence boundary: readable public text, bounded sample, discovery or unread body. Omission never establishes absence, and no authenticated account data is claimed. Individual unread URLs and tool errors explicitly leave the brand condition undetermined. These are observation limits, not additional read proof, a brand score or an automatic semantic classifier.

The same analyst is instructed to distinguish an actually sourced negative condition, an access/data gap and a qualified commercial hypothesis. Missing analytics access or a text extractor omitting tags cannot establish that the brand lacks measurement, has inefficient ads or converts poorly. Missing private revenue/margin data becomes a client question. A useful hypothesis keeps its concrete verification/measurement action instead of turning the report into generic data-missing prose. A source explicitly stating a negative condition can still support an appropriately scoped observation.

The review explicitly checks this distinction across narrative, highlights, SWOT and actions and applies only exact issue-linked patches. No heading/keyword score or fixed claim quota decides meaning. Evidence-boundary metadata cannot be used as a body quotation or counted source. Original body text, IDs, passage offsets and source-proof digests remain intact; the boundary survives saved-offer reuse and its source scope remains visible with retained quotations.

Historical Gemini helper/fallback fingerprints are checked for both prior source-presentation layouts, including full-context fallback receipts.
