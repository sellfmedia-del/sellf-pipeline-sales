## 3. Read-source coverage

Normal report admission requires four distinct, relevant read sources. Search snippets, navigation-only pages, excluded sources, tracking aliases and exact duplicate body text do not add coverage. Different pages may share a domain; there is no invented four-domain rule. Relevant means the analyst has connected a source to a research question, explained its relevance and supplied a short genuine body passage that was actually delivered. A read and relevance review in the same unobserved tool batch cannot certify it. Body fingerprints and delivered intervals are deterministic; semantic relevance is the analyst's judgment and still needs the later semantic-review correction.

All report citations must use reviewed read sources. Quotes from a hidden archive tail remain invalid until a real saved-source read exposes that interval. Adjacent/overlapping delivered intervals merge; omitted gaps stay unread. Gemini assistance returns the exact source windows it received, and final fallback/recovery retain their actual delivered windows. Reading proofs and original source digests survive quote-only saved-offer captures, so reopening an offer cannot promote incomplete research into a complete report.

## 4. Research completion

`review_research` records the analyst's objective, questions/answers, important public gaps and selected/rejected/pending candidates in the same existing conversation. Updates merge by stable identity; omitting an open question does not delete it. Question reclassification needs a reason and is recorded. Answered important public questions must retain their reviewed source support. Unavailable public answers require actual source/tool attempts and a reason; important unavailable answers keep the result partial. Client revenue, margin and budget questions are separate from public research and do not block it. General analysis may remain a qualified inference without inventing a source.

Normal writing waits while an important public gap or relevant pending candidate remains. An ordinary text response, a short output allowance or one unaffordable tool no longer closes funded research automatically. The analyst can choose a narrower call, another useful source or a free archive read. The existing final-report reserve, 5,000 kuruş aggregate cap, time and turn limits remain authoritative. At a real limit, available narrative and sources are retained for pricing with explicit partial quality; no new research budget, unlimited loop or automatic paid report repair is opened.

The review checkpoint itself is zero-cost. Any analyst/helper/tool work during a user-started real audit continues to be metered in the same ledger. This development and its tests execute no paid provider call or real-brand analysis.
